I'm a first-year PhD student in Computer Science at [Tulane University](https://tulane.edu/){: .highlight}, where I work as a Research Assistant in the [Hydroinformatics Lab](https://hydroinformatics.tulane.edu/){: .highlight} under [Dr.&nbsp;Ibrahim&nbsp;Demir](https://sse.tulane.edu/ibrahim-demir){: .highlight}.

I am interested in **Educational AI and AI Interpretability**. My work explores how AI can be used to create more interactive and personalized learning experiences. I am also interested in how AI systems learn and represent knowledge, and how we can better understand what happens inside these models.
